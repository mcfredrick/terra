## Open Source Projects

- **[OpenFAST/openfast](https://github.com/OpenFAST/openfast)** — NLR-supported whole-turbine and FAST.Farm simulation code for aeroelastic wind turbine analysis and wind farm layout optimization, widely used for grid-integration and reliability studies in wind energy engineering.

- **[pvlib/pvlib-python](https://github.com/pvlib/pvlib-python)** — Mature open-source Python library for simulating photovoltaic energy system performance, providing modeled irradiance, temperature effects, and component-level loss analysis essential for solar plant design and optimization.

- **[ESCOMP/CESM](https://github.com/ESCOMP/CESM)** — Community Earth System Model open-source framework for simulating atmospheric, oceanic, and land surface processes, providing baseline climate projections used in impact studies, policy-relevant modeling, and climate change attribution.

- **[CliMT/climt](https://github.com/CliMT/climt)** — Python-based climate modelling toolkit providing process-level parameterizations and atmosphere dynamics frameworks for idealized and comprehensive Earth system simulations, enabling flexible climate process research.

- **[xgcm/xgcm](https://github.com/xgcm/xgcm)** — Python package for grid mapping and finite-volume analysis of general circulation model output data, simplifying CMIP/CMIP6 data handling for climate diagnostics, attribution, and model comparison studies.

- **[Energinet-SimTools/MTB](https://github.com/Energinet-SimTools/MTB)** — Automation toolkit for grid compliance studies in PSCAD and Powerfactory, streamlining high-voltage DC/AC power electronics simulation and renewable integration validation for power system engineering.

Parallel to this, the [multisensor data fusion framework](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/08/28/multisensor-data-fusion-optimizes-traffic-flow-intersection-safety-delivering-cost-time-savings/) demonstrated for traffic digital twins can be reconfigured to fuse oceanographic sensor inputs (e.g., CTD, pH, DIC probes) into a site-specific digital twin, enabling continuous verification of storage integrity and rapid anomaly detection. Together, these approaches yield a practical pipeline: climate-scale projections inform deployment siting and design margins, while adaptive sensor fusion ensures operational reliability without energy-intensive purification. An engineer familiar with Python-based data handling and fusion logic can prototype a validated, decentralized carbon removal system grounded in measurable, trackable metrics.
