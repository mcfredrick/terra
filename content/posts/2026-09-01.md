## Research Worth Reading

**[Python-Fortran Hybrid Programming to Fuse AI and Physical Models: Examples of AI-LDA in climate and weather models (Hf2pMDA_v1.0)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29532)** — Presents Hf2pMDA_v1.0, a Python-Fortran hybrid framework for integrating AI components into climate and weather numerical models. An engineer would care because it demonstrates practical patterns for combining machine learning with physical simulations in existing pipelines.

**[From Rigid to Adiabatic: Canonical Regularization of AC Networks via Action-Angle Variables](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29129)** — Proposes canonical regularization of AC networks using action-angle variables to overcome limitations of traditional phasor-based analysis as grid-forming converter penetration grows. An engineer would care because it provides mathematical tools for analyzing modern power systems where rigid-network assumptions fail.

**[Minimizing Grid Interconnection Capacity Requirements for AI Data Centers: A Developer-Side Planning Framework with Onsite Resources and Workload Flexibility](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29359)** — Introduces ICP-AI, a framework for minimizing grid dependency in AI data centers through optimization of onsite generation, storage, and workload flexibility. An engineer would care because it addresses the interconnection bottleneck with computational planning approaches.

**[Stimulated Oscillations in Renewable Energy Integrated Power Systems - Part II: Methodology of Oscillation Mitigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29518)** — Develops systematic methodologies for damping high-amplitude oscillations in renewable-integrated power systems under small perturbations. An engineer would care because it provides concrete approaches for stability challenges emerging with high renewable penetration.

**[Uncertainty-Aware End-to-End AI Weather Forecasting: Disentangling Observation and Model Contributions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30795)** — Extends the Aardvark Weather model to probabilistic forecasting from raw observations while quantifying uncertainty from observation and model sources. An engineer would care because it replaces costly numerical weather prediction pipelines with scalable AI that maintains uncertainty quantification.

**[Diffusion-Based Refinement for Kilometer-Scale Probabilistic Precipitation Nowcasting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30205)** — Introduces exPreCast-ENS, a conditional residual diffusion framework upgrading deterministic radar nowcasters to 1km probabilistic systems with quantified uncertainty. An engineer would care because it demonstrates how diffusion models can enhance extreme precipitation prediction.
