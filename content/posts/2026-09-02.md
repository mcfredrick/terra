## Today's Synthesis

An engineer-actionable approach connects three recent technical advances: a Bayesian maximum a posteriori framework for uncertainty-aware condition monitoring of power converters [https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00218], vehicle-to-building (V2B) integration for data center peak shaving [https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00204], and the record-scale deployment of grid lithium-ion batteries across the U.S. [https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/batteries/us-built-more-grid-batteries]. A practical implementation would build a real-time dispatch layer that ingests converter health metrics from the Bayesian estimator to detect statistically significant parameter drift, then coordinates distributed V2B EV discharge and grid-scale battery storage to shave peak loads on data centers. The estimator prevents excessive cycling of silicon-carbide converters by flagging drift beyond normal variation, while the V2B layer taps parked EV batteries already situated near data center campuses, and the grid batteries provide bulk energy buffering for peaks exceeding EV capacity. This triad directly solves the reliability-flexibility trade-off in climate infrastructure, is implementable with existing telemetry and optimization stacks, and offers a concrete entry point for engineers trained in control, ML, or systems to contribute to grid decarbonization without introducing new failure modes.
