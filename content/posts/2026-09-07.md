## Research Worth Reading

- [Estimating the Health and State of Charge of Each Cell in a Second-Life Battery System from Field Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04487) — Joint SOH/SOC estimation for second-life cells where OCV curves are unavailable, using field data fused with an equivalent circuit model. Relevant for anyone building battery management or second-life reuse systems where cell-level diagnostics are required without full pack data.

- [Grid-Mode-Aware Model Predictive Control of Hybrid Energy Storage Systems for AI Data Center Power Smoothing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04398) — Grid-mode-aware MPC coordinating battery and supercapacitor storage to smooth variable AI data center loads under grid constraints. Engineers working on data center infrastructure or power electronics will recognize the control architecture as directly applicable to load-shaping problems.

- [Headroom-Aware Stochastic Adaptive Model Predictive Control for Load Frequency Control in Microgrids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05332) — Stochastic adaptive MPC for microgrid frequency control that dynamically quantifies inverter headroom instead of assuming fixed reserves. Useful for anyone designing adaptive controllers for high-IBR microgrids where inertia is declining.

- [Integrating Agentic Artificial Intelligence with High-Performance Computing for Grid Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04544) — AgentiGrid couples LLM-based autonomous agents with HPC to run iterative multi-scenario power flow studies for grid planning. A concrete example of LLM agents orchestrating simulation workloads — relevant for ML engineers looking to apply agentic patterns to infrastructure problems.

- [Regional Frequency Constrained Dispatch Method Considering Spatial-joint Stochastic Disturbances and Contingencies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05087) — Frequency-constrained dispatch that models regional frequency heterogeneity and spatially correlated stochastic disturbances, jointly optimizing generation and transmission. Addresses a real gap as renewables reduce system inertia; relevant for optimization/modeling engineers entering grid operations.

- [US Lobby's $1.1 Trillion Freight Rail Electrification Study Intentionally Asked The Wrong Question](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/06/freight-rail-electrification-study-wrong-question/) — Critique of a large rail electrification study that shifts focus to practical engineering: catenary placement, overhead line design, and operational constraints. Useful for engineers evaluating infrastructure electrification projects where the technical scope matters more than headline numbers.
