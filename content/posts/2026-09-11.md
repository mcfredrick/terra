## Research Worth Reading
- **[Impedance-Based Sensitivity Analysis for Stability Enhancement of LCC-HVDC Links Connected to Weak Grids Using Grid-Forming Converters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10774)** — Presents frequency-domain impedance-based sensitivity methodology for LCC-HVDC stability under weak-grid conditions, integrating black-box system identification with Generalized Nyquist Criterion. Enables systematic stability enhancement for grid-forming converters in low-inertia systems.
- **[KiloDA: Reconstructing kilometer-scale near-surface wind states from sparse station observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11230)** — Diffusion-based framework for hourly kilometer-scale near-surface wind reconstruction from sparse surface station observations. Learns statistical wind field structures over complex terrain for high-resolution wind resource mapping.
- **[A Counterexample to Two Representative Unit Aggregation Formulations for Unit Commitment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11139)** — Demonstrates PCUC and related unit aggregation formulations are not region-exact for slow-ramping units, with counterexample where aggregate trajectories lack feasible unit-level realizations. Highlights risks of naive aggregation in high-renewable unit commitment.
- **[Quantifying the Unintentional Islanding Risk: A Comparative Study on Active Distribution Network](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11400)** — Investigates dynamic behavior of medium-voltage active distribution network during grid-to-island transition, quantifying unintentional islanding risk amid high inverter-based resource penetration. Comparative analysis of protection schemes and control strategies offers practical guidance for distribution system operators.
- **[A Primal-Dual Formulation for Pricing Static Voltage Stability Services within a Unit Commitment Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11436)** — Proposes primal-dual framework to price static voltage stability services inside unit commitment, addressing gap for grid-following inverters on weak buses with low short-circuit ratios. Integrates voltage stability metrics into dispatch optimization for market-based stability services.
