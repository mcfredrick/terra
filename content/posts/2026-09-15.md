## Research Worth Reading
- [Low-Ripple Modulation Strategy for a Photovoltaic-Based Triple-Port Hydrogen Production System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13746) — Proposes a modulation strategy for a triple-active-bridge isolated DC-DC converter integrated with photovoltaic sources to enable stable, low-ripple hydrogen production via electrolysis. Addresses power quality and energy conversion efficiency barriers in green hydrogen systems, offering engineers a concrete power electronics and control framework for renewable-to-fuel integration.
- [Active Filter Design for Buffering Datacenter-Scale Power Fluctuations from Training AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13490) — Introduces an active power delivery architecture with advanced filtering to buffer datacenter-scale power ramps from large AI model training, stabilizing grid interaction and enabling higher compute density. Engineers interested in real-time control, grid-forming converters, and power quality mitigation will find the filtering and hardware-in-the-loop applications directly transferable.
- [From Winding-Fault Geometry to Reliability: Estimation and Prognosis of Stator Inter-Turn Faults](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13448) — Presents an integrated framework for estimating and prognosing stator inter-turn faults in induction motors, characterizing fault severity by the fraction of short-circuited turns and spatial orientation using a geometric fault model where the output signature is affine in the fault parameters. Enables condition monitoring and reliability assessment for electric motor systems, a key component in electrified transport and industrial applications.
- [Optimal Threshold Type Policies for Partially Observable Restless Bandits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13886) — Develops analytical optimization policies for PO-RMAB problems motivated by resource-constrained wildlife monitoring, providing closed-form threshold conditions to balance state revelation and monitoring costs. The decision-theoretic and bandit optimization framework transfers to climate tech resource allocation, such as sensor network design for environmental monitoring.
