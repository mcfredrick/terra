## Research Worth Reading
- **[Iterative Single-Loop Coordination of Transmission and Distribution Systems and DER Aggregators with Limited Information Sharing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21435)** — Proposes a transparent, decentralized single-loop iterative coordination method between TSOs, DSOs, and DER aggregators, designed for scalability with limited information sharing. The framework enables unified operational planning across grid hierarchy, supporting active management of distributed resources.
- **[A Distributed Step-by-step Finite-time Consensus Design for Heterogeneous Battery Energy Storage Devices with Droop Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21313)** — Develops a distributed finite-time consensus scheme for heterogeneous BESS units operating under droop control, enabling cooperative manipulation across geographically dispersed storage assets. The approach improves SoC balancing and grid support capabilities without centralized communication, key for fleets with mixed hardware.
- **[Optimal Day-Ahead Scheduling of Fast EV Charging Station With Multi-Stage Battery Degradation Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20946)** — Introduces a day-ahead scheduling framework for EV fast-charging stations equipped with BESS, using a novel multi-stage battery degradation model instead of single-model approaches. The method optimizes dispatch while explicitly tracking intertemporal SOC constraints and extending battery lifespan, relevant for charging infrastructure operators.
- **[Distributed Multiconsensus Control of BESSs Based on Centrality of Eigenvectors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21856)** — Designs a communication weight allocation method based on eigenvector centrality for connected directed graphs, enabling secondary and SoC balance control across distributed BESS units. The approach optimizes coordination topology without global state knowledge, enhancing storage fleet efficiency under partial observability.
- **[Optimal Regret for Online Storage Control via Cumulative Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21262)** — Formulates online control of a scalar storage system with adversarial arrivals and convex state-action costs, proposing cumulative policies that achieve optimal regret bounds. The framework is directly relevant to real-time energy storage dispatch under uncertain generation and load, supporting grid balancing with provable performance guarantees.
- **[Minimizing Bid Cost Recovery for Energy Storage with Uniform Pricing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21230)** — Studies uniform in-market pricing and out-of-market bid cost recovery payments for energy storage resources in rolling-window dispatch, addressing temporal SOC coupling that breaks standard LMP mechanisms. The work provides optimization insights for fair and efficient market clearing in high-renewable grids.
