## Research Worth Reading
- **[Merging Large Language Models and Battery Physics for User-Aware Electric Vehicle Driving Management](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27050)** — Hybrid framework integrating an LLM as a high-level behavioral reasoning layer atop a physics-driven battery supervisor. Bridges semantic driver intent with numerical battery models, relevant for engineers building intent-aware BMS.
- **[Reshaping Converter-Network Interactions in Microgrids: From Virtual Impedance to Virtual Two-Port Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27223)** — Replaces additive virtual impedance with a virtual two-port approach using four coordinated transfer channels. Improves dynamic interaction and steady-state power sharing in inverter-based microgrids.
- **[Holonic Graceful Transitions Across Centralized, Decentralized, Distributed, and Local Control in DER-rich Cyber-Power Distribution System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27759)** — Holonic framework enabling seamless mode transitions across control hierarchies under normal and fault conditions. Addresses adaptive coordination challenges as DER penetration grows.
- **[Evaluating Grid Strength with Rising Penetration of Inverter Based Resources](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27066)** — Analyzes voltage/power oscillations in Dominion Energy Virginia tied to high inverter-based generation. Quantifies relationship between system strength and inverter penetration for grid stability assessment.
- **[Forced Oscillations in Power Systems Induced by Data Centers Hosting AI Workloads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27698)** — Shows AI workloads can excite poorly damped oscillatory modes, threatening grid stability. Provides framework for assessing risk across varying system strength and damping conditions.
- **[The Climate Case for EVs Goes Deeper Than You Think](https://heatmap.news/electric-vehicles/ev-climate-benefits)** — Explores lifecycle emissions, regional grid decarbonization, and charging-pattern dependencies beyond tailpipe comparisons. Gives engineers a nuanced evaluation framework for EV adoption under varying grid mixes.
