## Research Worth Reading
- **[Safe Receding Horizon Mixed-Integer Differentiable Predictive Control for Degradation-Aware Battery Dispatch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28698)** — Mixed-integer differentiable predictive control framework for residential battery dispatch that combines neural-network speed with recursive feasibility guarantees and real-time state-of-charge feedback. Engineers familiar with MPC will recognize the control structure but should note the degradation-aware scheduling and sinusoidal time-of-day constraints as practical additions for battery systems.
- **[HClimRep-Ocean: A Global Ocean Emulator on an Unstructured Mesh](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28601)** — ML emulator for global ocean processes on unstructured meshes, targeting mesoscale eddies and kinetic energy that are hard to resolve in traditional forecasts. Relevant for ML/software engineers who want to accelerate climate models or build downscaling tools.
- **[Optimal Measurement Selection for Certifiable Voltage Monitoring in Power Distribution Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28785)** — Optimal measurement selection framework that certifies voltage compliance in distribution grids with high DER/EV penetration despite sparse real-time measurements. Power systems engineers and control theorists will appreciate the formal guarantees on voltage bounds under observability constraints.
- **[Adaptive State Estimation Under Topological Uncertainty in Unobservable Primary Distribution Systems Using Strategically Placed Sensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28830)** — Deep learning framework for adaptive state estimation in primary distribution networks with frequent topology changes and high DER integration. Addresses unobservability via strategically placed sensors, appealing to ML engineers working on real-time estimation with incomplete data.

## Today's Synthesis

If you're an engineer looking to apply ML/controls to climate impact, the intersection of differentiable predictive control and grid observability offers a practical entry point. The [Safe Receding Horizon Mixed-Integer Differentiable Predictive Control for Degradation-Aware Battery Dispatch](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28698) framework shows how neural-network-accelerated MPC can honor real-time state-of-charge feedback while explicitly minimizing battery degradation—a scheduling problem directly relevant to large-scale EV fleets and grid storage. Complementing this, [Optimal Measurement Selection for Certifiable Voltage Monitoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28785) provides formal guarantees on voltage bounds under sparse sensor networks, addressing the observability constraints that arise when integrating high DER penetration. Together, these two works form a control-observability stack: deploy a degradation-aware dispatch loop, then layer in certifiable voltage monitoring to ensure grid stability under limited measurements. This exact combination is what the [$1.9B DOE-funded advanced grid projects](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/transmission/advanced-grid-tech-doe-boost) are designed to support at scale, making it both a technically grounded skill set and a deployment-ready career path for engineers pivoting into climate infrastructure.
