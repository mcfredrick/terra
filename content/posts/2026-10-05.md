## Research Worth Reading

- **[Modular Model Order Reduction for Inverter-Based Power Systems with Stability and Accuracy Guarantees](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02477)** — Proposes a modular model order reduction framework for large-scale grids with renewables, allowing independent reduction of sub-areas while preserving interconnection structure and user-specified accuracy. It addresses the computational bottleneck of stability analysis in converter-dominated systems, which is directly relevant for control engineers working on grid integration.
- **[Distinguishing sodium-ion penetration and sustained transport in realistic hard carbon nanostructures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02411)** — Combines machine-learning-generated hard-carbon structures with molecular dynamics simulations of explicit electrolyte to map sodium-ion penetration against sustained transport. Relevant for battery material engineers seeking microstructure-transport relationships to guide high-performance anode design.
- **[Generation and Transmission Expansion Planning with BESS-Based Virtual Transmission Lines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02674)** — Presents a mathematical model for long-term planning that treats storage in place of transmission assets as relaxed virtual lines to mitigate congestion in deregulated markets. Offers optimization frameworks for engineers working on grid infrastructure and capacity expansion under policy and market constraints.
- **[An Analytical Review of Model Order Reduction Methodologies of Converter-Dominated Power Systems for Stability Studies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03059)** — Surveys model order reduction methodologies for converter-dominated systems, covering model fidelity, multi-timescale dynamics, and stability interactions across wide frequency ranges. Provides a structured entry point for engineers entering power systems stability research before implementing specific reduction schemes.
- **[SDECast: Probabilistic Weather Forecasting in Continuous Time with Neural SDEs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03313)** — Proposes a probabilistic weather forecasting framework using neural stochastic differential equations in continuous time to explicitly encode locality and temporal continuity. Addresses error accumulation in autoregressive rollouts, offering a continuous-time alternative for engineers building renewable generation forecasts.
