## Today's Synthesis

The convergence of grid-interactive efficiency devices and virtual power plant (VPP) frameworks creates a tangible entry point for engineers from software, ML, and controls backgrounds. [This hidden device could free up gigawatts of power for the US grid](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/energy-efficiency/hidden-device-free-up-gigawatts-grid) describes how emerging efficiency technologies and grid-interactive controls capture otherwise wasted capacity across the economy, while [Newsom finally says yes to virtual power plants](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/virtual-power-plants/newsom-yes-to-virtual-power-plants) establishes a regulatory framework for aggregating distributed solar, storage, and flexible loads at scale. For an engineer pivoting into climate, the immediate opportunity lies in building control algorithms that translate real-time grid signals into optimized load adjustments for heterogeneous assets. ML models predicting device responsiveness, combined with open-loop/closed-loop feedback loops, can maximize the gigawatts of wasted capacity mentioned in efficiency studies. VPP APIs and standardization efforts (e.g., OpenADR, OCPP) provide the communication primitives needed to coordinate thousands of edge devices without central bottlenecks. The technical challenge bridges device-level telemetry and grid-scale dispatch, requiring systems thinking around latency, data provenance, and incentive compatibility. This space is already operational—utilities are piloting VPPs to defer peaker plants, and the hardware layer for demand response is maturing rapidly.
