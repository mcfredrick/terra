## Technology & Innovation
- **[Ultium Cells to Bring LMR Prismatic Battery Cell Production to Tennessee for Future GM EVs](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/10/19/ultium-cells-to-bring-lmr-prismatic-battery-cell-production-to-tennessee-for-future-gm-evs/)** — Ultium Cells LLC, a GM-LG Energy Solution joint venture, plans to produce lithium manganese rich (LMR) prismatic battery cells at a new plant near Nashville, Tennessee. The shift to LMR chemistry aims to lower cobalt dependency and improve thermal stability, offering a practical pathway for cost-reduced EV battery packs at scale.
- **[Floating wind industry leans into global collaboration amid headwinds](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/offshore-wind/floating-turbine-global-collaboration-research)** — The article describes buoyant triangular steel or concrete platforms roughly the size of a baseball field that support wind turbines in deep-water offshore wind farms. It highlights a growing global research collaboration aimed at standardizing platform designs, optimizing mooring dynamics, and reducing levelized cost of energy for floating wind deployments.
- **[From Mine to Magnet to Motor: How GM Is Building Critical Supply Chains for the Long Term](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/10/19/from-mine-to-magnet-to-motor-how-gm-is-building-critical-supply-chains-for-the-long-term/)** — The article details General Motors' integrated supply chain strategy for permanent magnet materials used in electric motor production, tracing sourcing from mining through magnet manufacturing to final motor assembly. It highlights engineering challenges in securing critical materials like neodymium and dysprosium, and explores vertical integration as a strategy to de-risk EV motor production against material volatility.
- **[The World’s First Subsea Geothermal Well Had 8.7 Seconds of Glory](https://heatmap.news/climate-tech/endurance-energy-subsea-geothermal)** — About 300 miles off the Oregon coast, a mile beneath the ocean’s surface, Endurance Energy drilled what was claimed as the world’s first subsea geothermal well. The well achieved only 8.7 seconds of operational time before the experiment was concluded, underscoring the extreme engineering hurdles of sustaining well control and instrumentation in deep-ocean HPHT geothermal environments.
- **[A cleaner solution to America’s aluminum crunch is hiding in landfills](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/clean-aluminum/how-to-recover-more-scrap-metal-us)** — With U.S. aluminum supply strained by global shortages and tariffs, the piece explores recovering scrap metal from landfills as a cleaner alternative to energy-intensive new smelting. It outlines emerging material recovery techniques, including sensor-driven sorting and hydrometallurgical processing, and quantifies the potential energy savings of scaling landfill mining for industrial feedstock.

## Open Source Projects
- **[mohammadrezwankhan/zephyrtrade](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/zephyrtrade)** — zephyrtrade provides a Python framework for backtesting synthetic wind forecast strategies, asymmetric market offer decisions, and reproducible revenue models under electricity market rules. It uses scikit-learn and scipy for probabilistic forecasting and time-series optimization, offering a practical entry point for engineers to model renewable generation uncertainty and market participation strategies.

## Today's Synthesis

The probabilistic forecasting and time-series optimization framework **[zephyrtrade](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/zephyrtrade)**, which uses scikit-learn and scipy for wind forecast backtesting, offers a ready-made engineering pipeline for modeling renewable generation uncertainty. An engineer pivoting into climate work could adapt this framework to quantify energy yield and curtailment risk for the buoyant triangular platforms described in the **[floating wind collaboration](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/offshore-wind/floating-turbine-global-collaboration-research)** article, incorporating mooring dynamics and platform-standardization variables into the model's feature set. Beyond generation, the same data-assimilation and classification techniques can be redirected toward the **[aluminum landfill recovery](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/clean-aluminum/how-to-recover-more-scrap-metal-us)** piece, where machine learning models improve scrap-stream identification and boost material recovery rates. By refactoring the zephyrtrade codebase to handle sea-state sensor data or material-sensor data, engineers gain a transferable skill set that bridges wind market participation, offshore infrastructure optimization, and circular materials analytics—all without needing deep domain expertise upfront, and with immediate, measurable impact on levelized cost of energy and resource efficiency goals.
