## Open Source Projects
- [mlco2/codecarbon](https://github.com/mlco2/codecarbon) — codecarbon is an open-source library for tracking carbon emissions and energy consumption of Python workloads, providing metrics and visualizations to quantify the environmental impact of computational jobs and ML training pipelines.  
- [Zara-Toorox/Solar-Forecast-ML](https://github.com/Zara-Toorox/Solar-Forecast-ML) — SFML is a locally running solar forecasting model for Home Assistant built on an attention transformer architecture, enabling device-level solar energy prediction without cloud AI dependencies for privacy-preserving edge deployment.  
- [green-coding-solutions/green-metrics-tool](https://github.com/green-coding-solutions/green-metrics-tool) — The Green Metrics Tool provides software energy consumption and carbon emission measurement with timeline tracking and dashboard visualization, giving developers comparative baselines to optimize the carbon impact of codebases and CI/CD pipelines.

## Today's Synthesis

The intersection of California's EV charging reliability challenges and Ford's bidirectional EV architecture presents a concrete engineering opportunity: design a V2G-aware energy management system that uses real-time solar forecasts to optimize charge/discharge patterns for fleet vehicles. By integrating [codecarbon](https://github.com/mlco2/codecarbon) to measure the carbon footprint of the control software itself, developers can ensure that the efficiency gains from V2G integration don't come at an unacceptably high computational cost. Simultaneously, deploying [Zara-Toorox/Solar-Forecast-ML](https://github.com/Zara-Toorox/Solar-Forecast-ML) on edge devices in charging stations allows the system to predict solar availability and schedule bidirectional exchanges proactively, maximizing self-consumption of renewable energy. This creates a closed loop where solar forecast accuracy directly improves grid resilience, V2G economics, and total system carbon impact—an engineer actionable path that combines infrastructure systems design, power electronics optimization, and software observability into a single deployable feature.
