## Today's Synthesis

A practical synthesis: create a carbon-budgeted MLOps checklist for early DAC and grid-AI projects, then map permitting and transmission bottlenecks through assessments like [NRDC’s assessment of BAAJA, the 2026 Permitting & Transmission Bill](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/06/nrdc-assessment-of-baaja-the-2026-permitting-transmission-bill/). The engineer-actionable takeaway is to combine climate software, carbon-aware compute, and open-source sustainability tooling now, so when DAC hits commercial scale, the software layer is ready and the compute is accountable.
