## Open Source Projects
**[NatLabRockies/sup3r](https://github.com/NatLabRockies/sup3r)** — The repository provides a GAN-based super-resolution pipeline that downscales coarse ERA5 reanalysis data to high-resolution wind and solar spatiotemporal grids, preserving physical consistency while improving site-specific resource quantification. This bridges the gap between low-resolution climate model outputs and precision engineering feasibility studies for renewable project siting.
