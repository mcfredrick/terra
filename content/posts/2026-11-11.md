## Research Worth Reading — Veterans Day: Grid Reliability as Public Service

- [Risk-Aware Dynamic Operating Envelopes for Battery Arbitrage in Unbalanced Distribution Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13173) — This paper develops a risk-aware dynamic operating envelope framework for battery arbitrage in unbalanced three-phase distribution networks. It formulates the problem as a semidefinite program and derives sufficient conditions for exactness, which matters for engineers building deployable grid optimization tools.


- [Robust State-Parameter Estimation in Second-Order Thévenin Batteries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13202) — The paper estimates states and parameters of a second-order Thévenin battery model using measured current features. It identifies RC branch parameters during rest intervals via linear least squares, which is directly relevant to battery monitoring and energy-storage controls.

- [Modal-Augmented Chebyshev Physics-Informed Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks for Power Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13340) — This work introduces a physics-informed Kolmogorov-Arnold network architecture for power-system dynamic simulation. The modal-augmented Chebyshev formulation aims to capture electromechanical oscillations better than conventional physics-informed neural networks.

- [Direct Multispectral Satellite Nowcasting of Gridded Surface Solar Radiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12999) — The paper presents an observation-to-forecast framework that converts Himawari-8 multispectral imagery and solar geometry into gridded surface solar radiation nowcasts without numerical weather prediction. It targets high-resolution, frequently updated solar forecasts useful for solar energy and grid operations.

- [Impact of Reduced-Order Modelling on Sum-of-Squares Region-of-Attraction Certification for Grid-Forming and Grid-Following Converters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13497) — The paper studies how reduced-order modeling affects sum-of-squares certification of regions of attraction for grid-forming and grid-following converters. It addresses the computational burden of transient-stability analysis in power-electronic-dominated grids.

## Today's Synthesis

The three grid-side papers chain together as a forecast → constrain → verify pipeline, and each stage fails in a distinct way if you skip it. Start with the forecast: run [direct multispectral satellite nowcasting of gridded surface solar radiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12999) on Himawari-8 bands plus solar geometry to produce a gridded irradiance nowcast you refresh every few minutes, then convert it into a per-node PV-generation trajectory. Feed that trajectory into [risk-aware dynamic operating envelopes for battery arbitrage in unbalanced distribution networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13173): solve the semidefinite program each interval and check the exactness conditions before trusting the result, so battery commands stay inside per-phase voltage and thermal limits rather than merely maximizing arbitrage revenue. Because the envelope is only as trustworthy as the model underneath it, close the loop with [robust state-parameter estimation in second-order Thévenin batteries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13202): during rest intervals, run the linear least-squares identification to refresh RC branch parameters and states, then feed the updated model into the next solve so model drift doesn't silently invalidate the feasible region.

The outcomes are separate, and worth scoping separately: the nowcast buys accuracy on solar ramps, the envelope buys a hard guarantee that dispatch respects network constraints, and the estimator buys model validity over time — a forecast error, a constraint violation, and a stale model are three different failures with three different fixes. What none of the papers ships is the glue: wiring the nowcast into the optimizer, gating every dispatch against the certified envelope, and scheduling re-identification between operating intervals. That integration and safety layer is the part an engineer has to build, and it is where systems, controls, and software work add value on top of the published results.
