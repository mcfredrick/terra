Cryosphere work runs on exactly the stack most engineers already know: multi-decadal satellite imagery, change-detection pipelines, and mass-balance models that feed downstream water-supply forecasts.

## Technology & Innovation

- **[Perovskite Solar Cells Head for US Commercial Production](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/11/perovskite-solar-cells-us-caelux-heliene/)** — Caelux and Heliene are pushing perovskite and perovskite-silicon tandem cells toward volume manufacturing, with solution-processed absorbers that are cheap in material terms and have climbed in efficiency quickly since 2009. The interesting engineering is not the cell physics but the scale-up: deposition uniformity, inline metrology, and statistical process control on a thin-film line.
- **[The High-Rise Bottleneck: Why EV Charging in Indian Apartments Is an Engineering Puzzle](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/11/the-high-rise-bottleneck-why-ev-charging-in-indian-apartments-is-an-engineering-puzzle/)** — Shared parking, limited transformer headroom, and no dedicated home connection make the Western assumption of one charger per car break down. That turns the problem into multi-tenant load scheduling under a hard capacity cap, which is a constrained optimization and metering problem more than a hardware one.
- **[Running a Smart Home Energy System Takes a Lot of Software](https://heatmap.news/climate-tech/lunar-ev-charging)** — Coordinating a home battery, rooftop solar, an EV charger, and utility price or emissions signals means reconciling devices that speak different protocols and disagree about state. This is distributed systems work: telemetry ingestion, forecasting, setpoint control, and graceful degradation when the cloud link or the grid signal drops.
- **[Leading small modular nuclear reactor will be late and over budget](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/nuclear/small-modular-nuclear-reactor-late-canada)** — Ontario Power Generation confirmed the first BWRX-300 at Darlington will miss its October 2030 in-service date and exceed budget. First-of-a-kind nuclear construction is a useful reality check on deployment timelines, and the digital side of the build (instrumentation and control, simulation, safety-case tooling) is a real software surface.

## Open Source Projects

- **[GreenScheduler/cats](https://github.com/GreenScheduler/cats)** — A Python scheduler that defers batch jobs to hours when grid carbon intensity is low, reading intensity data from regional providers. If you own CI runners, training jobs, or ETL pipelines, this is the shortest path from "I want to cut compute emissions" to something running in production.
- **[pkuehnel/TeslaSolarCharger](https://github.com/pkuehnel/TeslaSolarCharger)** — Charges a Tesla against live PV surplus and tariff signals from Tibber or aWATTar, with Home Assistant as the control surface. A compact, readable implementation of solar-aware load shifting you can study before building your own.
- **[CharlesGillanders/homeassistant-alphaESS](https://github.com/CharlesGillanders/homeassistant-alphaESS)** — A Home Assistant integration over the official Alpha ESS API exposing PV generation, battery state, and household consumption. Useful reference for inverter and battery API shapes, polling design, and pushing local energy data into dashboards and automations.
- **[open-risk/awesome-sustainable-finance](https://github.com/open-risk/awesome-sustainable-finance)** — A curated index of sustainable-finance software, datasets, and libraries spanning carbon accounting, climate risk modeling, and disclosure workflows. Good orientation if your transferable skill is data engineering and you want to see which tools and data schemas the reporting side actually runs on.

## Policy & Regulation

- **[Bay Area finalizes rules to boost heat-pump water heater adoption](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/heat-pumps/bay-area-heat-pump-water-heater-rules)** — The Bay Area Air Quality Management District finalized implementation of its 2023 zero-NOx standard for residential water heaters and space heating sold in the region, which in practice routes new installs toward heat pumps. Rules like this create concrete demand for compliance tooling, permit and rebate workflow software, and the controls and load-modeling work that makes heat pumps manageable on a constrained panel.

## Today's Synthesis

The Bay Area's zero-NOx water heater standard will push a large installed base of [heat pump water heaters](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/heat-pumps/bay-area-heat-pump-water-heater-rules) into homes over the next few years, and almost none of that fleet is observable or controllable in software today. That's the gap. The pieces already exist for the other loads: [TeslaSolarCharger](https://github.com/pkuehnel/TeslaSolarCharger) shows solar-surplus load shifting against tariff signals, [homeassistant-alphaESS](https://github.com/CharlesGillanders/homeassistant-alphaESS) shows how to model an inverter and battery API, and [cats](https://github.com/GreenScheduler/cats) shows carbon-intensity-aware scheduling. What's missing is the same treatment for a thermal load, where the state of charge is a tank temperature and efficiency is a COP curve that degrades as ambient air gets colder.

Concretely: a Home Assistant integration for a common HPWH, a first-order thermal model calibrated from a week of temperature and power telemetry, and a scheduler that preheats during low-carbon, low-tariff, high-PV hours while holding a temperature floor for hygiene. Then layer on the panel constraint from the [high-rise charging problem](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/11/the-high-rise-bottleneck-why-ev-charging-in-indian-apartments-is-an-engineering-puzzle) — a hard shared capacity cap that turns this into constrained optimization rather than a hardware question.
