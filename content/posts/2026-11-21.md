## Today's Synthesis

A concrete opening for engineers is to build the data layer that connects [JA Energy Storage's battery passport platform](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/20/ja-energy-storage-launches-an-internal-battery-passport-platform-to-facilitate-compliance-with-eu-standards/) with the kind of consumer storage aggregation described in [Jackery & Every Electric's plug-in VPP](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/21/jackery-every-electric-transform-urban-energy-storage-with-plug-in-vpp/). A battery passport is not just a compliance record; it becomes useful when it can carry device identity, chemistry, warranty limits, cycle history, and consent state into dispatch and settlement systems. Engineers could implement a registry API that assigns stable asset IDs, records signed lifecycle events, and exposes read-only telemetry such as state of charge, temperature, and available power. The same service could support VPP participation by mapping device capabilities to market products, logging dispatch commands, and reconciling delivered energy for settlement. Access control matters here: homeowners, installers, aggregators, and regulators need different scopes. A practical first project is a small event-driven service with PostgreSQL, object storage for signed documents, and an audit trail, then integrate one battery model and one tariff or grid-service workflow.
