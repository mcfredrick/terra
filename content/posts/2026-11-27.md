## Today's Synthesis

Connect [China’s solar overtaking hydropower](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/solar/solar-no-2-china-grid) as the grid-mix scenario, [Congo’s renewed cobalt export ban](https://heatmap.news/am/nvidia-drc-cobalt) as the supply-shock input, and [UbiQD’s perovskite greenhouse glass](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/11/27/perovskite-solar-quantum-dots-lettuce-water-conservation-ubiqd/) as a deployable edge case. The engineer-actionable idea is an open-source, material-constrained decarbonization sandbox: ingest public grid-mix and capacity data, model storage bill-of-materials under cobalt-dependent, LFP, and sodium-ion chemistries, then run Monte Carlo scenarios where solar capacity grows while cobalt export policy tightens. Add a module for distributed agrivoltaic/perovskite deployments that scores energy offset and water savings for controlled-environment agriculture. Ship it as a CLI with reproducible notebooks and a small web dashboard, so teams can compare “build more storage” against “deploy more distributed PV plus chemistry substitution” under policy uncertainty. The output is not a forecast; it is a decision aid that makes material risk and grid-mix assumptions explicit. Start with one region, one storage chemistry comparison, and one greenhouse pilot, then expand the data adapters. This turns three headlines into a codebase that helps engineers test climate-tech deployment choices before committing capital.
