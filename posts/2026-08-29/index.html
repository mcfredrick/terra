Open Source Projects

  • OpenFAST/openfast — NLR-supported whole-turbine and FAST.Farm simulation code for aeroelastic wind turbine analysis and wind farm layout optimization, widely used for grid-integration and reliability studies in wind energy engineering.

  • pvlib/pvlib-python — Mature open-source Python library for simulating photovoltaic energy system performance, providing modeled irradiance, temperature effects, and component-level loss analysis essential for solar plant design and optimization.

  • ESCOMP/CESM — Community Earth System Model open-source framework for simulating atmospheric, oceanic, and land surface processes, providing baseline climate projections used in impact studies, policy-relevant modeling, and climate change attribution.

  • CliMT/climt — Python-based climate modelling toolkit providing process-level parameterizations and atmosphere dynamics frameworks for idealized and comprehensive Earth system simulations, enabling flexible climate process research.

  • xgcm/xgcm — Python package for grid mapping and finite-volume analysis of general circulation model output data, simplifying CMIP/CMIP6 data handling for climate diagnostics, attribution, and model comparison studies.

  • Energinet-SimTools/MTB — Automation toolkit for grid compliance studies in PSCAD and Powerfactory, streamlining high-voltage DC/AC power electronics simulation and renewable integration validation for power system engineering.
