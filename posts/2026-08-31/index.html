Research Worth Reading

  • Distributed Model Predictive Control for Optimal Consensus of Constrained Heterogeneous Multi-agent Systems — Proposes a distributed MPC framework that jointly optimizes control sequences and dynamically feasible consensus equilibria across heterogeneous multi-agent systems. The approach improves consensus performance and scalability for microgrid coordination, vehicle-to-grid networks, and other distributed energy systems where agents have different dynamics and constraints.

  • Exact branch-transfer criterion for common-mode Thomson heat cancellation in thermoelectric couples — Derives a branch-transfer criterion for canceling common-mode Thomson heat in thermoelectric leg pairs, improving the efficiency of solid-state heat pumps and power generators. The result provides a design rule for high-performance thermoelectric materials relevant to waste-heat recovery and sustainable cooling.

  • Compiling Chemical Knowledge into Executable Descriptors for Materials Prediction — Introduces CRISP, a large language model-assisted framework that treats representation construction as a rule-space exploration and compilation problem, converting natural-language chemical knowledge into executable descriptors. The method accelerates materials prediction for climate-critical applications like battery electrolytes and carbon capture sorbents.

  • Anytime Primal–Dual Certification of the Maximum Disturbance Radius in Robust MPC — This work introduces an anytime certification framework for adjustable-set robust MPC that quantifies a state-dependent maximum disturbance radius as a robustness reserve. The method provides real-time estimates of remaining reserve after finite out-of-set disturbances, enabling adaptive safety margins for uncertain energy systems.

  • A Moisture-Vorticity Theory for the Boreal Summer Quasi-Biweekly Oscillation — Develops a moisture-vorticity theory on a weak-temperature-gradient slow manifold to explain the boreal summer quasi-biweekly oscillation in the tropics, analyzed with GPT-5.5. The framework diagnoses divergent circulation from moisture anomalies and models rotational circulation via Rossby waves, offering a computable basis for tropical climate dynamics.

  • Application of the latent twins approach for clear sky retrieval from IASI observations — Presents a data-driven latent twins method for clear-sky atmospheric retrieval from IASI satellite infrared sounder observations, bridging physics-based and machine learning retrievals. The approach improves out-of-sample generalization for climate monitoring of atmospheric composition and radiative forcing.
