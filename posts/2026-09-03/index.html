Research Worth Reading

  • Dimension-Reduced ADP for Real-Time Microgrid Operation with Massive Air-Conditioning Loads under Multiple Uncertainties — Proposes a dimension-reduced approximate dynamic programming method for real-time microgrid dispatch with massive HVAC loads and multiple uncertainties (weather, price). Introduces a post-decision value function to capture the impact of current decisions, enabling scalable real-time scheduling and reserve provision.
  • A Control Co-Design Framework to Optimize Sustainability with Application to Microgrid-Driven Data Centers — Presents a co-design framework that jointly optimizes physical plant characteristics and controller parameters for sustainability, applied to microgrid-powered data centers. Demonstrates how integrated control and infrastructure design can reduce environmental impact while maintaining performance.
  • Continuous-Time Aggregation of Massive Flexible HVAC Loads Considering Uncertainty for Reserve Provision in Power System Dispatch — Formulates the aggregation of massive, flexible HVAC loads as a continuous-time optimization problem to provide intra-hour reserve provision for following rapid renewable generation variations. Explicitly accounts for outdoor temperature uncertainty while maintaining computational tractability and scalability.
  • Kilometer-Scale AI Downscaling of Atlantic Hurricanes with Generative Ensembles — An AI-based dynamical downscaling system that autoregressively converts 3-hourly low-resolution boundary forcings into hourly high-resolution fields for Atlantic tropical cyclones. Couples the core solver with a diffusion model to generate ensembles of hazard-relevant variables.
