  • Regional Frequency Constrained Dispatch Method Considering Spatial-joint Stochastic Disturbances and Contingencies — Frequency-constrained dispatch that models regional frequency heterogeneity and spatially correlated stochastic disturbances, jointly optimizing generation and transmission. Useful for engineers evaluating infrastructure electrification projects where the technical scope matters more than headline numbers.
