Research Worth Reading

  • Koopman Spectral Reduced-Order Modeling of Spherical Diffusion in Lithium-Ion Batteries — Develops an analytical Koopman spectral reduced-order model for solid-phase diffusion in the single-particle Li-ion battery model, enabling fast electrochemical state estimation without solving costly PDEs. Directly applicable to BMS and grid-storage optimization, this gives engineers a computationally efficient alternative to full physics simulations.
  • From Laboratory Aging Studies to Field Predictions: Quantifying Uncertainty in Battery Storage Lifetime Predictions — Applies an open-source probabilistic degradation framework with cell-to-system approximation to predict battery energy storage system lifetime, validated against real field data. The uncertainty quantification is critical for warranties, maintenance planning, and investment decisions in storage projects.
  • From Learning to Control: Data-Driven Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning for Multivariable Control in a Microalgae Bioprocess — Presents a data-driven multi-agent reinforcement learning approach for multivariable control of microalgae photobioreactors, targeting stable pH and dissolved oxygen regulation under strong coupling and environmental variability. Simulations show improved productivity for biofuel and biomass applications, relevant to engineers working on bioprocess control.
  • Optimal Dispatch of a Hydrogen-Colocated Renewable-Powered Desalination Plant — Derives an analytical dispatch framework for profit-maximizing simultaneous production of water, electricity, and green hydrogen in a renewable-powered desalination plant combining thermal and RO units. Results reveal trade-offs and coordination benefits for water-energy nexus sustainability, useful for systems engineers designing integrated renewable infrastructure.

Technology & Innovation

  • Denmark Can Produce 140% Of Its Electricity Demand From Wind Power…So What Happens To The Extra 40%? — Denmark achieved a record where wind power reached 140% of national electricity demand, raising technical questions about grid management, curtailment, storage, and integration of excess renewable generation. Engineers should examine the surplus power handling strategies as a case study in high-renewable grid integration.
  • Dig Energy completes first project with novel geothermal drilling tech — Dig Energy completed its first commercial project, digging seven boreholes for a geothermal heating and cooling system at Suffolk Construction headquarters using novel drilling technology. This marks a practical deployment milestone for a startup aiming to improve geothermal drilling economics.
  • How Windmills as Wide as Jumbo Jets Are Making Clean Energy Mainstream — Modern wind turbine designs have grown to rotor diameters exceeding 200 meters, rivaling jumbo jets, enabling significantly higher energy capture per turbine. Advanced materials and aerodynamics are driving cost reductions in offshore wind deployment, relevant to engineers in structural and materials design.

Open Source Projects

  • Energy Vault Scaled Gravity Storage. The Battery Benchmark Is Brutal. — Energy Vault’s Rudong gravity-storage project in China serves as a real-world test of long-duration storage, built with domestic supply chains for concrete, steel, motors, and power electronics. The article compares its performance and cost benchmarks against battery storage, offering engineers a concrete reference for gravity-based LDES system design.
  • NatLabRockies/sup3r — sup3r uses generative adversarial networks to perform super-resolution on renewable resource data, generating high-resolution wind and solar spatiotemporal datasets from coarse reanalysis inputs. This directly addresses a key bottleneck in resource assessment and grid integration planning for engineers working on renewable forecasting.
  • Coal-to-solar project lands a rare clean-energy win in Ohio — State regulators unanimously approved a 149 MW solar and 149 MW battery storage project at the Hamden Energy site in Vinton County, Ohio, repurposing a former coal location. The approval represents a technical and regulatory case study in brownfield redevelopment with grid-scale solar and storage integration.
