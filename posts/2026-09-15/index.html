  • From Winding-Fault Geometry to Reliability: Estimation and Prognosis of Stator Inter-Turn Faults — Presents an integrated framework for estimating and prognosing stator inter-turn faults in induction motors, characterizing fault severity by the fraction of short-circuited turns and spatial orientation using a geometric fault model where the output signature is affine in the fault parameters. Enables condition monitoring and reliability assessment for electric motor systems, a key component in electrified transport and industrial applications.
  • Optimal Threshold Type Policies for Partially Observable Restless Bandits — Develops analytical optimization policies for PO-RMAB problems motivated by resource-constrained wildlife monitoring, providing closed-form threshold conditions to balance state revelation and monitoring costs. The decision-theoretic and bandit optimization framework transfers to climate tech resource allocation, such as sensor network design for environmental monitoring.
