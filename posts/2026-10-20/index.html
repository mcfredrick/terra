It outlines emerging material recovery techniques, including sensor-driven sorting and hydrometallurgical processing, and quantifies the potential energy savings of scaling landfill mining for industrial feedstock.

An engineer pivoting into climate work could adapt this framework to quantify energy yield and curtailment risk for the buoyant triangular platforms described in the floating wind collaboration article, incorporating mooring dynamics and platform-standardization variables into the model’s feature set.
